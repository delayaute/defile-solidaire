THE WHY

A real impact project, born at emlyon

At emlyon, every student group has to build a real impact project tied to one of the UN Sustainable Development Goals.

THIS YEAR · SDG 15 Protecting life on land forests, soils, biodiversity

Our project: The Charity Fashion Show. We collect second-hand clothes, put on a fashion show, then auction off the looks. The funds go to a local environmental charity. First collection with Emmaüs.
